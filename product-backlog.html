Individual reflection

When the team circulated the updated backlog on 30 July 2026, I could connect the planning tasks to the wireframes I had shared for feedback on 23 May 2026. The rider and driver journeys need different screens, but both depend on a clear sequence of actions. Tasks such as identifying users, mapping journeys and designing wireframes helped me see why UX work should follow real user needs.

I learned that a product backlog makes the scope visible and helps the team discuss dependencies before development. If I repeated the process, I would link each wireframe to a specific backlog card and record feedback and acceptance criteria more consistently. I have not treated assignment to a card as proof that I completed it.
