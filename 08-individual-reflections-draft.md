## 2. User Stories and Tasks — Reflection

Wireframing helped me think about the RideConnect application from the perspective of its users. The project includes driver and rider interactions, such as registration, ride offers, requests and booking-related screens. By creating and sharing wireframes, I contributed to discussing how users might move through the application before all features were implemented. The group discussion about driver and passenger views shows why it was important to distinguish the needs of these two user types. I learned that a screen should be designed around a clear user goal, not simply around its appearance. The exact user stories I personally wrote or edited still need to be checked against Trello.
