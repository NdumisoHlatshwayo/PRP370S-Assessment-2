1. Agile Team Charter

Evidence

Source: Group document Agile Team Charter-1.docx (provided in the group materials; original file still needs to be uploaded or linked here).

The charter states the team's goal was to develop a carpooling mobile application and website for students and workers commuting in Cape Town. Its planned capabilities include registration/login, drivers posting trips, passengers searching and joining carpools, displaying trip details, and preventing seat overbooking.

My documented role: Wireframes Designer — responsible for creating wireframes for key screens and supporting a clear, usable interface.

Ways of working recorded in the charter: Microsoft Teams communication; meetings at 8 PM for approximately 30 minutes; regular stand-ups, planning, reviews, and retrospectives; shared decision-making and respectful feedback.

Important: These are the team's stated plans and agreements, not proof that each ceremony or feature was completed.
