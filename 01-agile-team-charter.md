# Agile Team Charter — RideConnect

## Evidence and project purpose

The group's Agile Team Charter sets out the proposed RideConnect carpooling mobile application and website for students and workers commuting in Cape Town. The planned scope includes registration and login, drivers posting trips, passengers finding and joining rides, trip details, and preventing seat overbooking. These are **documented requirements**, not proof that every feature was completed or tested.

## Team responsibilities and working agreements

My role in the charter is **Wireframes Designer**: preparing screen layouts and helping the team plan a clear user experience. The charter sets out Microsoft Teams as a communication channel, meetings around 8 PM for approximately 30 minutes, regular stand-ups, sprint planning, reviews, retrospectives, shared decision-making, and respectful feedback. These describe intended team practices; actual activity is supported separately by dated group records.

## Dated supporting evidence

- **23 May 2026:** I shared Figma wireframes and asked the group for feedback.

The [RideConnect Trello board](https://trello.com/b/BpT1NslS/rideconnect-app) provides task-planning evidence. The [Figma design link](https://www.figma.com/design/DH5XHxuZhfFo2z5jRjcE5f/Practice) provides a design reference. Neither link alone proves that all planned work was completed.

## Individual reflection

The charter helped me understand that my responsibility as Wireframes Designer was connected to the wider team's planning, rather than being an isolated design activity. Sharing wireframes for feedback on 23 May 2026 made it possible for other members to comment on how passengers and drivers would move between screens. When I volunteered for UX Design during the July sprint discussion, I could see how early interface planning connected to development tasks.

I learned that an Agile charter is most useful when its agreements are followed by clear communication and visible work. In future, I would make feedback requests more specific and record design decisions and their outcomes directly on the task board. I cannot confirm from the available records that every planned meeting or retrospective occurred, and I do not claim to have written the entire team charter.

## Evidence limitations

The available dated chat records support selected coordination and design activities, not attendance at every meeting. Original design files and task completion records may provide further evidence.
