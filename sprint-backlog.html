Individual reflection

I learned that breaking work into sprint-sized tasks makes it easier to see how user research, wireframes, implementation and testing depend on one another. Sharing Figma wireframes before the July sprint discussion helped me see the value of early feedback. Volunteering for UX Design gave me a clearer place within the wider workflow.

If I repeated the process, I would connect each design decision to a Trello card, an owner, acceptance criteria and a recorded review outcome. I do not claim to have completed every UX task or attended every team meeting.
