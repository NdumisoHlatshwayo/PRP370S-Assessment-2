# 5. Agile Logbook

## Evidence

The team charter specifies regular Microsoft Teams meetings and Agile ceremonies. A WhatsApp group conversation is also available as potential supporting material. **Neither should be presented as a verified daily logbook until entries and dates have been checked.**

### Logbook format to populate from genuine records

| Date | Meeting / activity | My contribution | Challenge / action | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| To verify | To verify | To verify | To verify | Link or screenshot |

### Evidence to add
- [ ] Actual dated meeting notes or activity records.
- [ ] Verified progress updates and blockers.
- [ ] Relevant screenshots, redacted to protect other people's private messages and contact details.
- [ ] Retrospective notes where available.

## Individual reflection — to complete

How did tracking work help me? What did I learn from progress updates and feedback? What would I change in future?
